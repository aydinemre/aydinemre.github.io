S1 (kısa cevap, kolay) — sl. 11–13#

Scheduler ile dispatcher arasındaki farkı açıklayın. Dispatch latency nedir? İlk Gantt örneklerinde bu gecikme neden sıfır sayılır ve gerçek bir ölçümü yorumlarken bu varsayım neden geri getirilmelidir?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). The scheduler applies a policy to eligible (ready) work and decides which task receives the CPU next; the next task need not be the one that arrived first, and the queue need not be a literal FIFO. The dispatcher is the mechanism that carries out that decision: it switches context (saves one task's state, restores another's), switches to user mode and jumps to the right place so the chosen task actually executes OSC ch5 sl. 9. "Selection policy and context-switch mechanism answer different questions." Dispatch latency is the delay in stopping one task and starting another. It consumes time without advancing the application's computation, and it can disturb useful cache state (an indirect cost). The first timelines set it to zero to isolate the policy decision. A real measurement includes it, so a stated switch cost must be restored before claiming anything about real performance (e.g. the useful fraction q/(q+s)).

S2 (doğru/yanlış + gerekçe, kolay) — sl. 16, 35#

Her ifade için doğru/yanlış deyin ve tek cümleyle gerekçe yazın. (a) "Response time'ı 0 olan bir işin waiting time'ı da 0'dır." (b) "CPU utilization %100 ise sistem iyi tepki veriyordur." (c) "Ortalama waiting time'ı en düşük politika, en adil politikadır." (d) "İki algoritmayı karşılaştırırken her algoritmaya en uygun iş yükünü seçmek meşrudur."

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) False. Response is arrival to first CPU service; waiting accumulates all time spent ready but not running. A job can start immediately, be preempted later and wait in the ready queue (response 0, waiting positive). (b) False. Utilization is busy time over elapsed time, a resource-use metric; one long job can keep the CPU 100% busy while short jobs wait behind it. (c) False. A low mean can hide a few very long waits; fairness asks how service is distributed, and deadline requirements cannot be inferred from the mean either. (d) False. Keep the workload and assumptions fixed when comparing, and mark every arrival, preemption and completion; otherwise the comparison measures the workload choice, not the policy.

S3 (hesap, kolay) — sl. 25–26#

Bir sonraki CPU burst'ü exponential averaging ile tahmin ediliyor: τ(next) = α × t + (1 − α) × τ(previous). Başlangıç tahmini τ0 = 12. Gözlenen ardışık burst'ler: 4, 8, 16. (a) α = 0.25 için τ1, τ2, τ3'ü hesaplayın. (b) α = 0.75 için aynısını yapın. (c) Son gözlemden (16) sonra hangi α daha hızlı tepki verdi? Bunun bedeli nedir? (d) α = 0 ve α = 1 ne anlama gelir? (e) Hesapladığınız τ3, bir hard real-time görevin worst-case execution time'ı (WCET) olarak kullanılabilir mi?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) τ1 = 0.25×4 + 0.75×12 = 10; τ2 = 0.25×8 + 0.75×10 = 9.5; τ3 = 0.25×16 + 0.75×9.5 = 11.125. (b) τ1 = 0.75×4 + 0.25×12 = 6; τ2 = 0.75×8 + 0.25×6 = 7.5; τ3 = 0.75×16 + 0.25×7.5 = 13.875. (c) α = 0.75 jumped from 7.5 to 13.875: a large α responds quickly but follows noise more closely. α = 0.25 only moved from 9.5 to 11.125: a small α smooths observations but adapts slowly to a sustained change. (d) α = 0 ignores new evidence (the estimate never changes); α = 1 uses only the latest burst. (e) No. An estimate is neither a known future burst nor a worst-case execution-time bound; here τ3 = 11.125 is even below the last observed burst (16). A hard guarantee needs a justified WCET.

S4 (hesap, kolay) — sl. 65#

Kararlı (stable) bir sistemde işler saniyede λ = 12 iş hızıyla geliyor. (a) Ortalama kuyruk bekleme süresi Wq = 250 ms ise kuyrukta bekleyen ortalama iş sayısı Lq kaçtır? Birimleri yazın. (b) Başka bir ölçüm, çalışan iş dahil sistemdeki ortalama iş sayısını L = 4.5 buluyor. Bu 4.5'i 250 ms ile aynı formüle koymak doğru mu? Sistemde geçen ortalama süre W kaçtır? W − Wq neyi gösterir? (c) Sunucu saniyede en fazla 10 iş bitirebiliyorsa bu hesaplar ne olur?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) Lq = λ × Wq = 12 jobs/s × 0.25 s = 3 jobs (jobs/s × s = jobs; units expose the meaning). (b) No. The boundaries must match: a count that includes running work goes with time in the system, not queue-only waiting. Mixing them would imply a meaningless rate of 4.5/0.25 = 18 jobs/s. W = L/λ = 4.5/12 = 0.375 s = 375 ms. W − Wq = 125 ms is the average time spent in service per job ek bilgi: interpretation of the difference under consistent boundaries. (c) Arrivals (12/s) exceed the maximum completion rate (10/s): the queue is overloaded and grows without bound, so it has no steady-state averages and Little's law gives no valid Lq or Wq.

S5 (açıklama, orta) — sl. 43–46, 62#

4 fiziksel çekirdekli ve çekirdek başına 2 hardware thread'li (SMT) bir makine düşünün. (a) İşletim sistemi kaç logical CPU görür? Bu, 8 bağımsız çekirdeğin throughput'u anlamına gelir mi? (b) Bir öğrenci taskset -c 3 ./cpu-work ile işini CPU 3'e sabitliyor (pinning) ve "CPU 3 artık bana ayrıldı" diyor. Doğru mu? (c) ps -o pid,cls,ni,psr,comm çıktısında PSR = 3 görmek neyi gösterir, neyi kanıtlamaz? (d) Tek ortak ready queue ile CPU başına kuyruk (per-CPU queue) arasındaki ödünleşimi açıklayın. Load balancing ile affinity neden ters yönlere çeker? NUMA buna ne ekler? (e) 4 runnable thread'i olan bir süreç aynı anda kaç logical CPU kullanabilir? Tek bir thread?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) 8 logical CPUs OSC ch5 sl. 41. No: a physical core and an OS-visible logical CPU are different units. Hardware threads on one core share execution resources; overlapping lets one stream proceed while another stalls (compute/stall), but two logical CPUs on one core do not guarantee the throughput of two independent physical cores. The gain depends on which resources they compete for. (b) False. Affinity constrains eligible CPUs; it does not reserve a CPU or remove interference: other tasks and interrupts may still run on CPU 3. (c) PSR is a sampled CPU identifier (where the task was at that instant). It is not proof of continuous execution there and not an exclusive reservation. (d) A common queue makes global selection conceptually simple but creates contention on one shared structure. Per-CPU queues reduce shared access and help locality but can become imbalanced. Load balancing moves runnable work to an idle CPU; affinity tries to keep a thread where its cache is warm, so migration can improve balance while losing cache locality. On NUMA, memory placement relative to the CPU adds another cost (remote memory). Scheduling domains group CPUs by shared hardware and locality. (e) A process with several runnable threads can use several allowed logical CPUs (here up to 4, limited by its affinity mask and other load). An individual thread executes on one logical CPU at a time.

S6 (hesap, orta) — sl. 37–39#

Tek CPU, nonpreemptive priority, geçiş maliyeti 0, aralıklar [start, end). Küçük sayı = yüksek öncelik. Eşit öncelikte önce gelen (daha erken arrival) seçilir.

İş Arrival Burst Öncelik
P1 0 5 3
P2 1 3 1
P3 2 8 4
P4 3 2 2
P5 4 4 2

(a) Gantt şemasını çizin; her işin waiting time'ını ve ortalamayı bulun. (b) t = 1'de en yüksek öncelikli P2 geldiği halde neden P1'i kesmez? (c) Aynı girdide "büyük sayı = yüksek öncelik" denseydi ortalama waiting kaç olurdu? (d) P3 hangi koşulda starvation'a uğrar? Aging ve aynı seviye içinde round robin yardımcı olur mu?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) At 0 only P1 is ready: P1 [0,5). At 5 the ready set is P2(1), P3(4), P4(2), P5(2): P2 [5,8). At 8, P4 and P5 tie at priority 2; P4 arrived earlier: P4 [8,10), P5 [10,14), P3 [14,22). Waiting (P1…P5) = (0, 4, 12, 5, 6), mean = 27/5 = 5.4. With one burst each and no preemption, response equals waiting. (b) Nonpreemptive: the running task keeps the CPU until its burst finishes or it blocks. A higher-priority arrival only affects the next selection (at 5). (c) Larger is higher: P1 [0,5), P3 [5,13), P4 [13,15) (tie with P5, earlier arrival), P5 [15,19), P2 [19,22). Waiting = (0, 18, 3, 10, 11), mean = 42/5 = 8.4. The numerical convention is part of the input; write it beside the table. (d) If higher-priority work keeps arriving, P3 is postponed indefinitely: starvation. Aging gradually raises the priority of waiting tasks, but the real progress guarantee depends on how fast priorities change, ties and the admitted higher-priority load. Round robin within one priority level does not stop starvation caused by higher levels.

S7 (açıklama, orta) — sl. 59–63, cpu-work.c#

(a) Bir öğrenci şöyle diyor: "Linux 6.6'dan beri EEVDF 'virtual deadline' kullanıyor, yani EDF yapıyor; normal bir sürece wall-clock deadline garantisi verir." EEVDF, EDF ve SCHED_DEADLINE'ı ayırarak düzeltin. (b) Nice aralığı nedir; nice 10 neyi değiştirir, neyi değiştirmez? SCHED_FIFO ile SCHED_RR farkı nedir? (c) Okumadaki deneyde iki cpu-work aynı CPU'ya sabitlenip biri nice 0, diğeri nice 10 ile başlatılıyor. Her birinin cpu_s ve elapsed_s değeri için tahmininiz nedir? Bu deney hangi iddiaları kanıtlayamaz?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) EEVDF selects eligible fair-class work using virtual deadlines and service accounting; these are fairness/latency mechanisms. A virtual deadline is not an application's promise to finish by a wall-clock deadline. Classroom EDF picks the job with the earliest absolute (real) deadline; under the single-CPU, preemptible, D = T model U ≤ 1 is its exact condition. Linux SCHED_DEADLINE is a separate policy that uses EDF with bandwidth reservation machinery. A normal fair-class process gets no deadline guarantee. (CFS and vruntime are historical background; the installed kernel version matters.) (b) −20 to +19. Nice influences relative service, not a hard completion deadline; raising nice to 10 lowers the worker's priority and needs no privilege. SCHED_FIFO and SCHED_RR use static real-time priorities; RR time-slices peers at the same priority, FIFO does not OSC ch5 sl. 55. Linux real-time priorities use the opposite numerical direction to the textbook example. (c) Both have the same CPU demand, so cpu_s ≈ 0.75 s for each (slightly above, because the clock is checked only after each batch of arithmetic). Under contention elapsed > CPU time. The nice-0 worker is likely to accumulate its 0.75 s first (shorter elapsed); the nice-10 worker takes longer in elapsed time, and once the first finishes the survivor faces less competition. The experiment can reveal contention and relative service; it cannot prove an exact CPU-share ratio, identify the scheduler from timing alone or establish a hard real-time bound. A single run is not enough: repeat, reverse the launch order, compare with two equal-nice workers, and record uname -r, lscpu, the allowed CPUs and VM vCPU allocation (launches are not exactly simultaneous; background load, power management and compiler behaviour also matter).

S8 (hesap, orta) — sl. 17, 9, 21#

Tek CPU, FCFS (nonpreemptive). Ready queue, işin kuyruğa (yeniden) girdiği zamana göre sıralanır. I/O cihazı ayrıdır ve istek hemen başlar (cihaz kuyruğu yok). Geçiş maliyeti 0, aralıklar [start, end).

İş Arrival Davranış
P1 0 CPU 3 → I/O 4 → CPU 2
P2 1 CPU 5
P3 2 CPU 2

(a) CPU Gantt şemasını ve P1'in I/O aralığını çizin. (b) Her iş için turnaround (T), waiting (W) ve response (R) değerlerini bulun. P1'in waiting'i hangi aralıktan oluşur? (c) Bir öğrenci P1 için W = T − (toplam CPU) = 7 buluyor. Hata nerede? (d) [0, 12) aralığında CPU utilization ve throughput nedir? Bu utilization değeri iyi tepki süresi anlamına gelir mi?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) CPU: P1 [0,3), P2 [3,8), P3 [8,10), P1 [10,12). P1's I/O: [3,7). P1 re-enters the ready queue at 7, behind P3 (which entered at 2), while P2 is still running. (b) P1: C = 12, T = 12, CPU = 5, blocked = 4, W = T − CPU − blocked = 12 − 5 − 4 = 3, the ready interval [7,10); R = 0. P2: C = 8, T = 7, W = 2, R = 2. P3: C = 10, T = 8, W = 6, R = 6. Mean W = 11/3 ≈ 3.67, mean T = 9. (c) With I/O, turnaround also contains blocked time. 7 = 3 (ready) + 4 (blocked on the device); subtracting only CPU time counts device waiting as ready-queue waiting. Match the accounting formula to the workload model. (d) The CPU is busy for all of [0,12): utilization 12/12 = 100%; throughput = 3 jobs / 12 units = 0.25 jobs per unit. Multiprogramming overlapped P1's I/O with P2's CPU work; it did not make the device faster. 100% utilization does not imply good responsiveness: the 2-unit job P3 waited 6 units behind longer work.

S9 (tasarım + hesap, orta) — sl. 30–33#

Tek CPU'da 5 görev sürekli runnable, eşit öncelikli ve CPU-bound; dışarıdan yeni varış yok. Round robin kullanılıyor. (a) Geçiş maliyeti 0 iken q = 20 ms için bir görevin iki turu arasındaki en büyük boşluk kaç ms'dir? (b) Her görevin iki turu arasında en fazla 50 ms geçmesi isteniyor. q en fazla kaç olabilir? (c) Geçiş maliyeti s = 0.5 ms olsun. Yoğun (saturated) dönüşümlü çalışmada q = 12.5 ms ve q = 2 ms için yararlı iş oranı yaklaşık kaçtır? (d) (a)'daki sınırı hangi durumlar geçersiz kılar? (e) "Quantum küçüldükçe ortalama turnaround hep iyileşir" ifadesini ve "q çok büyükse RR, FCFS'ye yaklaşır" ifadesini değerlendirin. (f) Ready kuyruğunda tek görev kaldığında quantum dolması bir görev değişimi (switch) gerektirir mi?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) Gap ≤ (n − 1)q = 4 × 20 = 80 ms. (b) 4q ≤ 50 ⇒ q ≤ 12.5 ms. (c) Useful fraction ≈ q/(q + s): 12.5/13 ≈ 96.15%; 2/2.5 = 80%. A smaller quantum spends a larger share switching. (d) The bound assumes n continuously runnable equal peers, zero overhead and no outside arrivals. Switching cost, higher-priority work, additional tasks or a changing ready set (arrivals, tasks that block and return) invalidate it. (e) The first is false: turnaround versus quantum is not monotonic, because jobs finish at different boundaries and change later queue positions. The second is right with conditions: if q exceeds every burst in an all-ready, single-burst workload, each job finishes on its first turn in queue order, i.e. FCFS; with arrivals, blocking or other policy layers, restate the conditions. A tiny quantum wastes time switching; a textbook rule of thumb is not a universal Linux setting. (f) No. If no other task is runnable, quantum expiry need not cause a task-to-task switch; chart boundaries mark possible preemption points, not actual switches.

S11 (iz, orta-zor) — sl. 40–42#

Hocanın MLFQ öğretim politikası: Q0 round robin q = 8, Q1 round robin q = 16, Q2 FCFS. Yeni iş Q0'a girer. Bir seviyedeki quantum'unu tamamen kullanıp bitmeyen iş bir alt kuyruğa iner. Üst kuyruk önceliklidir: Q0'a yeni iş gelirse Q1 ya da Q2'de çalışan işi hemen keser. Kesilen iş kendi kuyruğunun başına döner ve o seviyedeki quantum'unun kalanını korur. Aynı kuyruktaki iş, çalışan işi kesmez. I/O yok, geçiş maliyeti 0, aralıklar [start, end).

İş Arrival CPU ihtiyacı
A 0 30
B 4 6
C 12 20
D 30 4

(a) Her aralığın hangi kuyrukta çalıştığını gösteren Gantt şemasını çizin. (b) Her işin completion, turnaround, waiting ve response değerlerini ve ortalama waiting'i bulun. (c) Alternatif kural: kesilen iş kuyruğunun sonuna gider ve o seviyede sonra taze, tam bir quantum alır. Ne değişir? Bu, sl. 42'deki "policy'yi kandırma (gaming)" ve "toplam pay (total allotment)" fikriyle nasıl ilişkilidir? (d) A tek başına çalışsaydı ne zaman biterdi?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) A@Q0 [0,8) (B arrives at 4 into Q0 but does not preempt a Q0 job); A demoted to Q1 with 22 left. B@Q0 [8,14), finishes. C@Q0 [14,22), demoted to Q1 with 12 left. A@Q1 [22,30): D arrives at 30 and preempts it (A has used 8 of its 16, 14 left). D@Q0 [30,34), finishes. A@Q1 [34,42) uses the remaining 8 of its quantum, still has 6 left → Q2. C@Q1 [42,54), finishes. A@Q2 [54,60). (b) Completion A 60, B 14, C 54, D 34. Turnaround = (60, 10, 42, 4). Waiting = T − CPU = (30, 4, 22, 0), mean = 56/4 = 14. Response = (0, 4, 2, 0). The last completion, 60, equals the total demand 30 + 6 + 20 + 4: no idle time. (c) At 34 Q1 = [C, A]: C@Q1 [34,46) finishes; A gets a fresh 16 and runs [46,60), finishing in Q1; it never reaches Q2. C completes at 46 (W = 14), A still at 60, mean waiting 12. A received 8 + 14 = 22 units at Q1 although the level allows 16, because the interruption reset its budget. A job that yields just before demotion could keep a high level the same way. A complete implementation must define this: account for the total CPU allotment at a level across runs, and define I/O return, promotion and periodic boost rules. (d) 8 in Q0 + 16 in Q1 + 6 in Q2: it completes at 30. Queue changes alone do not add useful work (zero overhead).

S12 (kavram + iz, orta-zor) — sl. 49, 58#

Tek CPU, preemptive sabit öncelik: H > M > L. Kilit işlemleri sıfır sürer. Aralıklar [start, end).

  • L, t = 0'da gelir: 1 birim çalışır, kilidi alır, kritik bölümde 3 birim çalışır, kilidi bırakır, 1 birim daha çalışır.
  • H, t = 2'de gelir ve hemen aynı kilidi ister; kilidi alınca 1 birim çalışır, bırakır, 1 birim daha çalışır.
  • M, t = 3'te gelir, kilit kullanmaz, 6 birim çalışır.

(a) Priority inheritance olmadan Gantt şemasını çizin. H ne zaman biter, kilidi kaç birim bekler? (b) Priority inheritance ile aynısını yapın. (c) "H'nin önceliğini daha da yükseltmek sorunu çözer" ifadesi neden yanlış? H beklerken hangi durumdadır? (d) Priority inheritance gerçek zamanlı bir garanti için yeterli midir?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). (a) L [0,3) (takes the lock at 1; at 2 H arrives, requests the lock and blocks, so L continues). At 3 M (higher than L) preempts L: M [3,9). L [9,10) finishes its critical section and releases the lock at 10; H wakes and preempts: H [10,12). L [12,13). H completes at 12, blocked from 2 to 10 = 8 units; 6 of them come from M, which does not even use the lock. This is priority inversion: medium-priority work indirectly delays high-priority work through the lock owner. (b) At 2 L inherits H's priority. M arrives at 3 but cannot preempt L: L [0,4), releases at 4 and drops back to its own priority. H [4,6), M [6,12), L [12,13). H completes at 6, blocked only 2 units (the rest of L's critical section). (c) H's priority alone cannot make the lock available; the dependency passes through L. While it waits for the lock H is blocked (waiting), not ready, so no selection rule can run it; only the owner's progress helps. (d) No. Inheritance temporarily raises the owner's priority, but full bounds still depend on the locking rules and critical-section lengths. Deadline reasoning also needs bounded interrupt latency, handler work, dispatch delay and nonpreemptible sections, not only a scheduler's selection rule. The simple RTA recurrence excludes blocking.

Son olarak (e): bir scheduling politikasını değerlendirirken deterministic timeline, simulation ve measurement her biri hangi soruyu cevaplar?

Cevabı ve ölçütü göster

Cevap (ideal). 1. Best according to which criterion, with what arrival information and burst knowledge? SJF minimizes average waiting only for a fixed set of jobs available together, with known bursts and zero overhead. Counterexamples: with arrivals over time nonpreemptive SJF must commit to a long job already running (S10: SJF 4.5 vs SRTF 4); bursts are only estimated; repeated short arrivals can starve a long ready job; round robin gives the long job earlier first service. Correct: "SJF minimizes mean waiting under those assumptions; choose a policy by the stated objective." 2. False: the slides' Example B has U = 0.9375 and misses at 80 (S13's C3 = 25 set also misses with U ≈ 0.946). The Liu–Layland bound n(2^(1/n) − 1) is sufficient, not necessary; exceeding it does not prove failure either (the harmonic set (1,2), (2,4) has U = 1 and meets every RM deadline). Use RTA for the model; EDF's U ≤ 1 holds for D = T only. 3. False: affinity restricts placement but permits other work and interrupts on the same CPU; it is not a reservation, and PSR is only a sample. Correct: "pinning makes competitors meet on a chosen CPU; it does not isolate it." 4. False: EDF orders jobs by application (absolute, wall-clock) deadlines; EEVDF's virtual deadlines are service-accounting devices for fairness and latency among fair-class tasks. Linux SCHED_DEADLINE is the separate EDF policy with bandwidth reservation. (e) A deterministic timeline answers a precise question for a stated workload: easy to check, narrow in scope. A simulation varies arrivals and burst distributions, but its output depends on the model and input traces and includes only the modeled mechanisms. Measurement includes real overhead and interference but needs reproducible conditions. In all three, keep the workload and metric fixed and compare more than the mean.
